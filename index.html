About

I run product like a GM. The roadmap is one piece of it. The customer, the team and the numbers are the rest.

I'm a product leader, founder and operator. For 18 years I've been taking messy, high-stakes problems and turning them into products that scale, mostly in consumer, fintech and AI.

I've done it inside big companies (MoneyLion / GEN Digital, Fidelity Investments, CVS Health, Reebok / adidas Group and Kaspersky Lab) and right alongside early-stage founders.

The approach doesn't change with the size of the company. Stay close to the customer, move fast, let the data settle the debates, and have some fun doing it.

Things I know well: product strategy, 0 to 1, growth, AI products, fintech, consumer, B2B and B2C, personalization, monetization, go-to-market, data and experimentation, UX and UI, Agile and Scrum, and leadership and operations.
