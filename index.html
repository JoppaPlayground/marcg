Q&A

Questions, answered.

Who is Marc Gigliotti?

Marc J. Gigliotti, known as The Product Guy, is a product executive, studio founder and seasoned builder with 18+ years across consumer products, fintech, AI and B2B. He has worked at MoneyLion / GEN Digital, Fidelity Investments, CVS Health, Reebok / adidas Group and Kaspersky Lab, and led MoneyLion's consumer product suite to a $1B acquisition by GEN Digital.

What does Marc Gigliotti do now?

He is VP of Product at MoneyLion / GEN Digital, founder and CPO of Joppa Digital, a product management advisory and operating partnership, and co-founder and CPO of Unicorn Product Studios, which works with founders and investors from 0 to 1, 1 to 10 and 10 to 100. He is also open to working with 1 to 2 founders or investor-backed startups as a fractional CPO or strategic advisor.

What has Marc Gigliotti built?

50+ products, including MoneyLion Discover, Credit Monitoring, Community and Active Investing; ReebokONE, a platform for 150k+ fitness professionals across 20+ countries; and, as product lead, FidelityGo and Fidelity Personalized Planning and Advice. Current work includes Forsured, Trendsendent and EducatorIQ.

Where has Marc Gigliotti worked?

MoneyLion / GEN Digital (VP of Product, 2022 to present), Fidelity Investments (Director, Product and Strategy, 2018 to 2022), Kaspersky Lab (Head of Digital Marketing and Product, North America and Latin America, 2017 to 2018), CVS Health (Senior Manager, Digital Experience Production and Operations, 2015 to 2017) and Reebok / adidas (Senior Manager, Digital Product Owner, Global, 2011 to 2015).

Where did Marc Gigliotti go to school?

He holds an MBA in General Management from the Manning School of Business at UMass Lowell and a BS in Business Administration from Salve Regina University. He is also a Certified Scrum Product Owner.
